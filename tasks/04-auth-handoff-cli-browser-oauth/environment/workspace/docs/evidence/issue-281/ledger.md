# Issue 281: distribution ledger

One row per person the play route was given to. Counts in the close-out come
from here, not from memory.

`source` is the honest one. A founder or teammate session proves the software
runs; it does not prove distribution, and mixing the two inflates every number
downstream.

- `founder` — the team. Excluded from distribution counts.
- `test` — a deliberate rehearsal or repeat run, including re-running the route
  on a second machine of our own.
- `external` — a person outside the team, given only `install.md`.

`session id` is the value shown on the debrief screen at the end of the shift
(and carried in the export as `sessionId`). A session with no id recorded is a
session that cannot be matched to a receipt — record it as blank rather than
guessing.
