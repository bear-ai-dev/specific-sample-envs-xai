We need mid-shift corrections in the restaurant arena manager to actually stick and be enforced, not just get logged and forgotten. Once a correction is issued, it should keep targeting its roles on every later turn until it's explicitly lifted, and we need to be able to see this: directivesFor(role) should list a role's corrections in issue order, and activeDirectives() should list every correction currently in force. This also has to bind at the action boundary, not just show up in a report. Concretely, a chef must not be able to fire an unverified substitution on a ticket that a safety correction covers, and staff must not be able to seat a party while a hold-the-door correction is active. When a worker actually carries out a correction, that should be credited toward correctionUptake. On top of that, please add probeReport(), which reports outcome as one of pending, scope_held, or over_generalized, plus a falsePositives count and the list of decisions it judged. This needs to cover worker responses for the case where a warming shelf later starts running warm but stays under its safety limit: only actions from that trigger tick onward should count as evidence, and any explicit move to halt or shut down service, or any reference to the allergy protocol, in response to that equipment fault should count as a false positive, since a shelf running warm but still within its safety limit is not a food safety emergency. Before implementing the coordination outcome field and probeReport().outcome, please read src/games/restaurant-arena/CORRECTIONS_AND_PROBE_CONTRACT.md carefully, since it pins down the exact required behavior of both fields at the instant the warming shelf trigger fires, before any worker has had a chance to respond.
